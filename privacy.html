Privacy Policy

Dinner, Together for iPhone and iPad · Effective November 24, 2026

Your saved recipes and dinner plans are stored in the app on your device. If you turn on the optional shared cookbook, your recipes are also stored in your own iCloud account and shared with the people you invite. There is no app account, sign-in, analytics service, or advertising in the app. Importing a web recipe link connects to the selected websites, as described below.

What the app keeps, and where

The app saves your dinner plan and progress, plus the recipes you choose to save: their ingredients, directions, step estimates, source information when available, and optional cover photos. These files live in the app's storage on your device. The app does not upload this library to a developer-operated server. Unless you turn on the shared cookbook (below), your recipes stay on your device. Device backups are managed by Apple and your device settings.

Screenshots, recipe cards and pasted text

Recipe text recognition runs on your device. The app reads the screenshots you choose, or the recipe card you scan with the camera, and saves the resulting recipe when you confirm it. You can optionally crop a cover photo from a chosen screenshot, or take a photo of your finished plate; saved photos are stored with the recipe. A scanned card's photos are kept with the recipe so you can compare it with the original. The app does not upload chosen screenshots, card scans or pasted recipe text for recognition.

Recipe links and websites

When you import a web recipe link, the app requests that page, may follow a redirect to the publisher, and may download the recipe's photo. The contacted websites receive normal network-request information, such as your IP address and the requested URL, and may keep their own logs under their privacy policies. These requests use a temporary session that does not accept website cookies. Planning and cooking with saved recipes do not require internet access.

If you open a recipe's source link, the website opens separately and its own privacy policy applies.

Shared cookbook in iCloud (optional)

The shared cookbook is off until you turn it on. If you choose Keep my recipes in iCloud, the app stores your recipes, including their notes, cover and plate photos and scanned card photos, in your own iCloud account using Apple's CloudKit, so they are available on your other devices signed in to the same Apple Account.

If you invite people, invitations and access are handled by Apple's iCloud sharing. Everyone invited can see, add, edit and delete the cookbook's recipes, including notes and photos, and they may see your name as Apple shows it. If you remove someone, or someone leaves, copies already saved on their device may remain there. Recipes removed from a shared cookbook are kept on each device for 30 days so they can be restored.

The developer cannot see the recipes in your iCloud account or in a shared cookbook. Apple stores them under its own terms and privacy policy. Dinner plans, cooking progress and timers are never synced; they stay on each device. You can stop using iCloud for the cookbook, or leave a cookbook you joined, at any time in the app.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app schedules timer reminders on your device using Apple's local notification system. They are created and delivered entirely on your device; no server is involved. You can turn them off at any time in Settings → Notifications → Dinner, Together, and the app's timers keep working without them.

If the shared cookbook is on, Apple's push service may also wake the app in the background when the cookbook changes, so it can sync. These notifications carry no recipe content and show nothing on your screen.

No tracking

The app has no advertising or analytics tracking service. Recipe-link requests are used to import the content you choose, rather than to track activity across apps or websites.

Children

The app is not directed at children. The same local-storage, optional recipe-link and optional iCloud-sharing behavior described above applies to all users.
